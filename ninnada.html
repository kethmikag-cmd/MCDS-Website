What is Ninnada'26?

Ninnada is the trademark event of the Media Circle of D.S.Senanayake College. It is the largest and most prestigious all-island trillingual media competition and day in Sri Lanka, well-recognized for representing the most talented young media enthusiasts across the country. The judging panel will feature renowned media personalities to ensure a high-quality competitive experience and fair evaluations. The Overall Champions of Ninnada will receive the Ninnada'26 Championship Trophy, while the Best School Student Media Personality will be awarded the prestigious R.I.T. Alles Memorial Shield.
